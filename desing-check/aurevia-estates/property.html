About the residence

Set on twenty katha at the edge of the Malnicherra tea gardens, this three-level villa was designed by a Dhaka studio for a family returning from London. The south-east orientation brings soft morning light into the double-height living room, while deep verandahs keep the afternoon heat and monsoon rain at bay.

The ground floor opens to a 14-metre pool terrace and a mature garden of jackfruit, mango and frangipani. Upstairs, five en-suite bedrooms look across to the tea estate; the master suite has its own study and a covered balcony facing the hills. A separate guest quarter with kitchenette sits over the four-car garage.

Finishes are restrained and well made: Burma teak doors, honed Italian marble in the living areas, and Grohe fittings throughout. Osmani International Airport is six minutes away, which makes this a rare home for families who divide their year between Sylhet and the UK.

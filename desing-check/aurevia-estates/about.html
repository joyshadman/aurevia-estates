About Aurevia

A Sylheti agency, built on a handshake.

Aurevia began in 2012 with one advisor, one rented office above Zindabazar, and a promise to families abroad: we would treat their home in Sylhet as if it were our own.

Our story

Our founder, Mahbub Alam, spent a decade in London watching relatives buy homes in Sylhet sight unseen — and too often regret it. He came home and opened Aurevia to do the opposite: fewer properties, checked by hand, sold with complete honesty about their faults as well as their charms.

Fourteen years later we are a team of eighteen, and we still turn away most of the homes we are offered. The ones we keep have clear title, an approved plan, and an advisor who knows the street, the neighbours and the way water moves in July.

  1. 2012

    First office opens above Zindabazar with one advisor.

  2. 2016

    London desk launches for British-Bangladeshi buyers.

  3. 2021

  4. 2026

Our mission

To make buying a home in Sylhet feel as certain from Manchester as it does from Mirabazar.

Candour

Craft

Continuity
